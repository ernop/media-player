media-player

Run demo/make-media.sh once to make the media. Press ? in a player for its keys.

Events from the players show here.

Video with two kinds of markers

The legend's buttons hide or show a kind. T widens the page, S saves the frame, R repeats, Shift+J and Shift+L jump by halves.

Audio in parts

The buttons beside play, Shift+P and Shift+N (and a headset's track keys) move between the parts.

Two cameras on one clock, without sound

A source: the player drives both videos as one, and with R starts both again at the end.

Audio in a box you can resize

Drag the lower right corner: the scale and controls follow the box's width. The page wrote loop, so repeat starts on.
